How to get your Canvas calendar file
  1. On a laptop, open Canvas and click Calendar in the left menu.
  2. At the bottom of the right sidebar, click Calendar Feed and copy the link.
  3. Paste the link into a new browser tab. It downloads a file ending in .ics.
  4. Come back here and choose that file below.

If your browser tries to open it in a calendar app instead, try Chrome. Canvas doesn’t say what you’ve turned in, so check things off here as you finish them.

Brain dump

Type every worry, to-do, and random thought out of your head. Don’t organize it. Once it’s written down, you don’t have to keep holding it.

If stress or worry is sticking around for days, or getting in the way of school, sleep, or friends, talk to your school counselor, a parent, or another adult you trust. You don’t have to figure it out alone.

If you ever feel unsafe or think about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) any time, day or night.
